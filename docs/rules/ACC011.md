# ACC011: Missing required reviewer team

**A merged change fails when its risk tier requires a reviewer team and no member of that team approved the merged head before merge.**

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Rule ID | `ACC011` |
| Policy key | `missing_required_reviewer_team` |
| Default severity | `high` (SARIF level `error`) |
| Enabled by default | yes, and inert until a policy's [tiers](../policy.md#risk-tiers) name `required_reviewer_teams` |
| Specification | [AI Change Provenance §6.2](../../spec/ai-change-provenance.md#62-rules), tiers in [§6.7](../../spec/ai-change-provenance.md#67-risk-tiers), collection in [§4.3](../../spec/ai-change-provenance.md#43-reviewer-teams) |
| Conditions and fixtures | [docs/policy.md](../policy.md#exact-deterministic-conditions) |

## Why it matters

Some changes need a particular kind of reviewer, not just a second pair of eyes: a change to
authentication needs someone from security, a migration needs someone who runs the database. A
risk tier says which changes those are, and this rule checks that the people the tier names were
the ones who approved.

## What counts

For each team the change's applied tier requires, an approval of the current head, at or before the
merge, that is the reviewer's latest decision, by a human who is a member of the team and is not
the effective human, with evidence at the policy's `minimum_review_evidence`.

Membership is the team's **as observed at collection**, not at review time: forges expose current
membership only. A member who left after approving is not counted; one who joined afterwards is.
The manifest records when membership was observed (`events.teams[].observed_at`).

## Outcomes

| Outcome | When | Reason text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `fail` | A required team has a complete membership record and none of its members approved. | "No qualifying approval of the current head before merge by a member of TEAM." (every such team, comma-separated) |
| `pass` | Every required team approved. | "A member of every required reviewer team approved the current head before merge." |
| `unknown` | The effective human is unknown or below the evidence minimum, reviews are incomplete, or a team that did not approve has an incomplete membership record. | "Effective human author is unknown; independence cannot be established."<br>"Effective human author's evidence is below the policy minimum; independence cannot be established."<br>"Review collection is incomplete; required team approvals cannot be established."<br>"The membership of TEAM could not be read in full; whether a member approved cannot be established." |
| `not_applicable` | The change is not merged, or its tier requires no team. | "Change has not been merged."<br>"No reviewer team is required for this change's tier." |

**Incomplete data never passes.** A merged change whose tier requires a team without a complete
membership record makes the collection incomplete: `acc scan`, `acc check` and `acc evaluate` exit
`4`.

## Collecting the facts

`acc scan` and `acc pr` collect the members of every team the policy's tiers require;
`acc export --team ORG/SLUG` collects them for a later `evaluate`. They come from
`GET /orgs/{org}/teams/{slug}/members`, which needs organization **members read** access. The
workflow `GITHUB_TOKEN` cannot be granted it; use a GitHub App installation token or a
fine-grained token with *Members: read*.

## Controls

- SOC 2 (2017 TSC) CC8.1: changes are authorized under change-management procedures
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A: A.8.32 Change management; A.5.3 Segregation of duties
- NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5: CM-3 Configuration change control; CM-4 Impact analyses

The mapping is a guide for control owners, not a compliance claim.

The change's tier is `high`, which requires `acme/security`; two humans approved, neither a member.


## How to fix

- The change is already merged: have a member of the team review it, and record a disposition.
- Prevent recurrence by requiring the team in CODEOWNERS for the paths that route a change to the
  tier; [change-risk](https://github.com/noru-tech/change-risk)'s requirements document lists them.

## Recording a disposition

A finding is never deleted. To record a human decision about it, edit only the finding's
`disposition` in the manifest (`accepted`, `remediated` or `false_positive`, with `owner`,
`decided_at`, `rationale`, and `remediated_at` or an optional `expires_at`), then run
`acc check MANIFEST --as-of YYYY-MM-DD`. Exact rules: [policy and dispositions](../policy.md#recording-exceptions).
